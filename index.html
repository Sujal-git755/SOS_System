Privacy Architecture

1. User-Controlled Location Sharing:

Location data is only transmitted when you explicitly start a journey or trigger assistance.

2. Controlled Approximate Corridor:

Exact GPS coordinates are never broadcast publicly. Helpers receive a 250m grid buffer to safeguard personal privacy.

3. Auto-Expiration & Zero-Retention:

Journey corridor broadcasts immediately end when you arrive or end the trip.

4. Rigorous Helper Verification:

Community members must submit verified institutional IDs and sign safety conduct agreements before receiving pings.
